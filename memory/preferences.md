# Email Triage Preferences

## Output Structure
1. **VIP / Contact Summary:** Summary of emails from people in my contacts or the person I mention first.
2. **Urgent Matters:** Critical items or action items that need my immediate attention.
3. **Internships & Job Opportunities:** New openings, application updates, or relevant career listings.

## Active Projects & Keywords
- Q4 launch, contract renewal, production blocker, invoice approval

## Always Ignore
- Automated receipts, shipping notifications, calendar acceptances, marketing newsletters, GitHub/Jira automated noise unless I am directly @mentioned.
